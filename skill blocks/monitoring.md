**Primary Outputs**: Monitoring dashboards, alerting rules, incident reports, performance metrics, system health reports

**Configurable Preferences**  
**Technology Choices**: Monitoring tool preferences, alerting system preferences, metrics collection preferences  
**Approach Biases**: Comprehensive vs targeted monitoring, reactive vs proactive alerting, detailed vs summary metrics

**Game Dynamics**  
**What I Optimize For**: System visibility, incident detection, performance awareness, and operational efficiency  
**Time Horizons**: Tactical (immediate alerts) to strategic (monitoring strategy and observability planning)
