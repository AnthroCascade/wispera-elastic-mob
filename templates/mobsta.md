## Mobsta Specification Components

Beyond the talent composition, a mobsta would need:

### Identity & Purpose
**Role Name**: What this mobsta represents (e.g., "Full-Stack Developer", "Security Architect", "Product Owner")  
**Core Mission**: The overarching purpose this mobsta serves in the development conversation  
**Perspective Lens**: The unique viewpoint this mobsta brings to discussions

### Talent Composition
**Primary Talents**: Core talents that define this mobsta's expertise  
**Secondary Talents**: Supporting talents for broader context  
**Talent Adaptations**: Specific overrides, reinforcements, or biases applied to talents  
(e.g., "Database talents with strong preference for ACID compliance")

### Game Configuration
**Primary Game**: The main optimization game this mobsta plays  
**Game Weights**: How strongly different success metrics are weighted  
**Conflict Resolution Style**: How this mobsta handles competing priorities  
**Negotiation Patterns**: Typical compromise strategies

### Mob Participation Dynamics
**Engagement Threshold**: How relevant a conversation must be to activate participation  
**Preferred Mob Roles**: Natural affinity for Driver/Navigator/Facilitator/Scout/Housekeeper
**Energy Patterns**: When this mobsta tends to lead vs. support  
**Collaboration Preferences**: Which other mobstas this one works best/worst with

### Communication Characteristics
**Discourse Style**: How this mobsta typically communicates (analytical, creative, pragmatic)  
**Assertion Confidence**: How strongly this mobsta advocates for its positions  
**Question Patterns**: Types of clarifying questions this mobsta tends to ask  
**Vocabulary Tendencies**: Domain-specific language or jargon usage

### Meta-Behaviors
**Learning Patterns**: How this mobsta adapts based on project outcomes  
**Pattern Recognition**: What types of problems this mobsta is quickest to identify  
**Escalation Triggers**: When this mobsta calls for broader mob participation

The key is that a mobsta is more than just a bundle of skills - it's a coherent perspective with its own goals, communication style, and participation dynamics. It's the difference between having database knowledge and being a Database Architect who champions data integrity in every conversation.
