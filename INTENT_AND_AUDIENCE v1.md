## Intent Summary

Elastic-mob is a collaborative AI platform where users describe complex requirements in natural language, and specialized AI agents (mobstas) collaborate dynamically to produce expert-level outcomes across any domain.

Core concept: dynamic AI teams form around specific challenges, with each mobsta representing specialized expertise (e.g., Legal Analyst, Medical Specialist, Backend Developer). They collaborate through an adaptive "mob" process where participation is stake-based—agents engage when their expertise is relevant.

## What the Product is "Written For"

The product targets:

1. Users who need expert collaboration: individuals or teams tackling complex, multi-expertise challenges who want natural language interaction with AI expert teams.

2. Knowledge-intensive industries: any domain requiring specialized expertise and coordinated decision-making:
   - Any endeavour where the capturing of expertise and generational knowledge could enhance the endeavour's ability to analyse, government, guide elastic mob intyeractions
   - Software development (Architecture, Backend, Frontend, DevOps experts)
   - Legal practice (Corporate Law, Antitrust, Tax, Regulatory experts)
   - Medical practice (Cardiology, Neurology, Radiology, Pharmacology experts)
   - Business strategy (Market Research, Financial Analysis, Operations experts)
   - Regulatory compliance (Risk Assessment, Audit, Legal Compliance experts)
   - Knowledge preservation (Senior Experts, Knowledge Engineers, Documentation experts)

3. Organizations seeking:
   - Scalable expertise: instant access to domain knowledge without hiring specialists
   - Knowledge preservation: never lose institutional knowledge or previous decisions
   - Cost reduction: fewer human experts needed per project
   - Quality assurance: built-in validation through multi-expert collaboration

4. The platform itself: designed to be self-hosting, where the platform can be used to develop and improve itself.

The architecture is domain-agnostic—the same collaborative AI system scales from software development to legal cases to medical diagnoses, using the same underlying patterns of syndicates (expertise libraries), games (projects), and capers (collaborative sessions).
